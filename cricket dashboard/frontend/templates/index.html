Methodology & Mathematical Scoring

Scientific approach, data transformation pipeline, and metric weighting.

🏏 Batter Reliability Score (BRS) Formula
BRS = [ 0.45 * (Pressure_SR / 160) + 0.30 * (Pressure_Boundary_Pct / 22) + 0.25 * (Pressure_Avg / 35) ] * 100 * Sample_Shrinkage

Where Sample_Shrinkage = min(1.0, sqrt(Balls_Faced / 75)) guarantees small samples do not artificially jump to Rank 1.

🎯 Bowler Reliability Score (PRS) Formula
PRS = [ 0.40 * max(0, (12 - Pressure_Econ) / 5) + 0.35 * (Dot_Ball_Pct / 45) + 0.25 * (Pressure_Wkts_Per_Over / 0.35) ] * 100 * Sample_Shrinkage

Rewards low economy under pressure, high dot ball percentages when batters are attacking, and critical breakthrough wickets in the death overs.

About This Project

Project Objective: The primary mission of this project is to create an objective, evidence-based cricket analytics platform capable of isolating genuine clutch performance from superficial batting and bowling averages. By analyzing real IPL and T20 International data from 2022 onwards, we determine who the most reliable match-winners truly are when games are on the line.

Competition Context: Built exclusively for the SupeRR Selector Season 2 challenge. It incorporates modern sports data science techniques including Bayesian sample normalization and situation-weighted scoring.
